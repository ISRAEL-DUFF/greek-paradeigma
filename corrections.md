## Presentation decisions pending founder/book verification

1. **Macrons:** long ᾱ is written with macron (τέχνᾱς, χώρᾱ) matching H&Q's printed
   paradigms. Long υ/ι in λύω etc. is NOT marked (precomposed ῡ + acute doesn't exist;
   combining-character rendering is unreliable in GFS Didot). Confirm this split is
   acceptable or decide to drop macrons entirely.
2. **Movable ν:** displayed as `(ν)` — λύουσι(ν), ἔλυε(ν) — per build-plan open
   decision #1's recommendation.
3. **Vocatives:** omitted from all declension tables for now (v0 presentation).
   H&Q prints them — founder to confirm whether they enter as rows or as tier-3 cells.
4. **Units 2–6 inventories are provisional:** all placements were made by inference
   (imperfect/χώρᾱ/ἄνθρωπος/δῶρον → 2; aorists/θάλαττα/PP λείπω → 3; M/P
   present+imperfect/ὁδός → 4; future mid/aorist mid/φύλαξ → 5; perfect
   system/δαίμων/ἐλπίς → 6; PP παιδεύω → 1). The definitive unit→content mapping
   must be transcribed from the physical book (build plan §5.2) before the
   `unitMappingVerified` flags flip to true. Unit 1 follows the founder's v0 scope.
5. **Pluperfect endings:** authored with H&Q's older-Attic set (-η, -ης, -ει(ν),
   -εμεν, -ετε, -εσαν). Other texts print -ειν/-εις/-ει — confirm against the book.
6. **First-aorist segmentation:** stored as σ (tenseMarker) + α-set endings so every
   cell has a distinct ending chip; H&Q presents the marker as -σα-. If the founder
   wants σα as one piece, 1st sg would need an empty ending chip — decide before
   authoring more aorists.
7. **Perfect 3rd plural:** authored λελύκᾱσι(ν) with macron per the ᾱ policy above.
8. **Units 7–8 also provisional:** perfect M/P, aorist passive, σῶμα, PP λύω → 7;
   future passive, γένος, ῥήτωρ → 8.
9. **γένος (σ-stems) uses a surface split** (γέν + ους etc.) rather than underlying
   γενεσ- + contraction rules; the two-step sandhi (σ-drop, then vowel contraction)
   belongs to the M6 authoring pass alongside contract verbs. Note in the paradigm
   explains the contraction.
10. **σῶμα nominative:** underlying σῶματ- with final-τ drop stored as a sandhi
    rule, so the blank scaffold shows the true stem (σῶματ—) and the "no ending"
    chip completes it — same convention as δαίμων/ῥήτωρ ν/ρ-stem nominatives.
11. **Units 9–14 also provisional:** ποιέω → 9; τιμάω/δηλόω → 10; subjunctives → 11;
    M/P subjunctive + optative → 12; αὐτός → 13; τίς → 14. Subjunctive/optative
    placement in particular needs the book — H&Q may introduce moods earlier.
12. **Contract verbs** store underlying stem + ending with a per-cell contraction
    sandhi rule (ποιέ+ομεν, έο→οῦ). Blank scaffolds therefore show the underlying
    stem (ποιέ—) and reveals show the contracted surface — M6's collapse animation
    will make that transition explicit.
13. **Accent finishing move (M5)** asks acute/circumflex on vowel slots only:
    breathings and iota subscripts are shown, never asked; grave never occurs in
    citation forms. αὐτάς (acc. fem. pl.) is written without a macron because no
    precomposed ᾱ+acute exists (see item 1).
14. **λύω subjunctive homograph webs** (λύω/λύσω/λύῃ/λύσῃ) are linked across
    indicative, subjunctive, and principal-parts tables; reverse lookup only
    requires same-table homographs — cross-table ambiguity drills remain a
    future candidate (all-tables lookup).
15. **Units 15–20 also provisional:** δίδωμι → 15, τίθημι → 16, ἵστημι → 17,
    εἰμί/ἵημι → 18, δείκνυμι/οἶδα → 19, φημί/εἶμι → 20. H&Q's real μι-verb
    sequence must come from the book. ἵημι present is authored from the
    reference (all six forms attested exactly); its AORIST (ἧκα, εἷμεν…) is
    still unauthored — the reference doesn't cover it, and we don't ship
    single-sourced forms anymore.
16. **εἰμί imperfect 1st singular** authored as ἦ; H&Q also lists ἦν (which would
    homograph with the 3rd singular). Confirm the book's presentation.
17. **Irregulars use drillClass "wholeForm"** — suppletive single-piece cells
    drilled as whole words (chips are forms, never endings). Level-2 assembly and
    the accent stage do not apply to them by design.
18. **THE STANDING REQUIREMENT (build plan §Phase 4 acceptance):** every μι-verb
    table (δίδωμι, τίθημι, ἵστημι, and ἵημι when added) must be hand-verified
    against the book by TWO people independently before the provisional flags
    flip.
19. **Second-source cross-check is now wired** (`npm run crosscheck`, report in
    `crosscheck-report.md`): the reference corpus is Mastronarde's Ancient Greek
    Tutorials paradigm pages in `../resources/` (three saved by the founder from
    the old Berkeley site, four fetched 2026-08-06 from the live atticgreek.org:
    paradigmsVerbs1–3 + paradigmsAdjectives). As of 2026-08-06: **360/364 forms
    attested** — 213 exact including accents (ALL μι-verbs, irregulars, contract
    verbs, pronouns, and 3rd-declension nouns among them), 147 via model-stem
    transposition (βουλεύω/γνώμη/ἔργον/πρᾶγμα/ἀσπίς/ἤγαγον/ἐλῶ — accent check
    rides on the human pass). Zero scattered single-cell flags anywhere.
    The only 4 unattested forms are PP λείπω's lexeme-specific parts (λείπω,
    λείψω, λέλειμμαι, ἐλείφθην) — no reference model exists for them; they go
    to the book check. The adjectives page is saved for the future participle/
    adjective authoring pass.
